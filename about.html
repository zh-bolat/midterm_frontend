About Forma

Designed slowly.
Built to last.

We are a small team of designers and carpenters from Astana who believe a home needs fewer, better things.

Our story

From a small workshop to 12,000 homes.

Forma started in 2019 in a rented garage on the edge of Astana. Two friends — a designer and a carpenter — wanted furniture that was simple to look at, honest about its materials and strong enough to be passed down.

Today we design every piece in our studio, build it with local craftspeople and sell it directly from our showroom. No middlemen, no fast trends, just furniture you will keep.

What we believe

Four simple rules.

  • Fewer, better pieces

    We release one small collection a year and keep every model in production for a long time.

  • Honest materials

    Solid oak, ash, wool, linen and leather. No veneers pretending to be something else.

  • Made to be repaired

    Screws instead of glue, removable covers and spare parts for every model we have ever sold.

  • Fair to people

    Our craftspeople are paid above the city average and work normal hours, all year round.
